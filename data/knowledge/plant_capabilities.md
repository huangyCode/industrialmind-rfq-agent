# Plant Capabilities

We manufacture in three plants. All plants work to the same quality system (ISO 9001) and the same
routing and inspection standards. Hourly rates are kept in the work center master data.

## Overview

| Plant | Location | Base lead time | Overhead | Logistics to DE customers | Typical strengths |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DE | Augsburg, Germany | 3 weeks | 12 % | 1 % | precision shafts, gears, prototypes, urgent orders |
| PL | Wroclaw, Poland | 4 weeks | 12 % | 3 % | medium series, aluminium flanges and housings, assemblies |
| CN | Suzhou, China | 9 weeks incl. sea freight | 12 % | 8 % | large series, simple turned and milled parts, gears |

- Add 1 week to the base lead time when an operation is outsourced (heat treatment, anodizing, plating).
- Air freight from CN reduces lead time to 5 weeks at about 4 % additional logistics cost; not default.

### Plant DE (Augsburg)

- 8 CNC lathes up to Ø320 mm × 800 mm, two with driven tools and sub-spindle.
- 6 machining centres (4 × 3-axis, 2 × 5-axis), max. part size 800 × 600 × 500 mm.
- 2 cylindrical grinders (Ø3 – 250 mm, length up to 650 mm, IT5 achievable), 1 surface grinder.
- Gear hobbing up to module 6 and Ø300 mm, gear quality 7 per ISO 1328.
- Climate-controlled measuring room with 2 CMMs; first article inspection reports.
- Preferred for: IT6 and finer, prototypes (≤ 20 pieces), urgent orders below 4 weeks, engineering changes.

### Plant PL (Wroclaw)

- 12 CNC lathes up to Ø250 mm, bar feeders for Ø10 – 65 mm (unattended third shift).
- 10 machining centres (7 × 3-axis, 3 × 5-axis) with pallet changers, aluminium high-speed spindles.
- 1 cylindrical grinder (Ø5 – 200 mm, length up to 500 mm), 1 surface grinder.
- No gear hobbing: gears and splines for PL assemblies are made in DE or CN.
- Assembly area with 6 workstations and functional test benches.
- Preferred for: series of 200 – 5000 pieces, aluminium parts, sub-assemblies.

### Plant CN (Suzhou)

- 15 CNC lathes up to Ø400 mm, 8 machining centres (3-axis only).
- No cylindrical grinding and no 5-axis milling. Parts with IT6 diameters or Ra ≤ 0.8 µm on
  diameters cannot be finished in CN.
- 3 gear hobbing machines up to module 4, gear quality 8 – 9.
- Surface grinding available for flat parts (plates, flanges).
- Preferred for: series above 1000 pieces with stable design and lead time of 9 weeks or more.

## Lead times

| Situation | DE | PL | CN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard machined part | 3 weeks | 4 weeks | 9 weeks |
| With outsourced heat or surface treatment | 4 weeks | 5 weeks | 10 weeks |
| Prototype (≤ 20 pieces), expedited | 2 weeks | 3 weeks | not offered |

- If no plant meets the requested delivery date, quote the fastest feasible plant and state the
  earliest possible date.

## Plant selection

1. Exclude plants that lack a work center of the routing.
2. Exclude plants that cannot meet the requested delivery date.
3. Choose the lowest unit price among the remaining plants; on a tie prefer DE.
4. If no plant is left after step 2, recommend the feasible plant with the shortest lead time.
